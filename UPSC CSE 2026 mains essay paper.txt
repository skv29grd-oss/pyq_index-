UPSC CSE 2026 MAINS ESSAY PAPER

Time: Three Hours                                                Maximum Marks: 125

Instructions:
Write two essays, choosing one topic from each of the following Sections A and B,
in about 1000-1200 words each. (125 x 2 = 250 marks)

Contents indicate the sequence of ideas, expression, and quality of thoughts expressed.

SECTION A

Choose ONE of the following topics for your essay (in about 1000-1200 words):

1. Oxymorons reflect the ironies of life.
2. A grateful mind is very beautiful.
3. A thorn is a changed bud.
4. When two elephants fight, it is the grass that gets trampled.

SECTION B

Choose ONE of the following topics for your essay (in about 1000-1200 words):

1. Nature is the symbol of the spirit.
2. A well-educated mind will always have more questions than answers.
3. Shelving hard decisions is the least ethical course.
4. A good leader is one who follows the followers.
