# UPSC CSE syllabus map for the Syllabus Tag column

Tag format: `Paper › Subject › Topic › Subtopic`. Paper, Subject and Topic must be copied exactly from one line below; Subtopic is the specific concept or institution in the news (short, e.g. `Air pollution — GRAP, CAQM`). Use the Mains paper when the item has Mains value; use `Prelims` only for Prelims-only facts (places, species, awards, exercises). verify.py parses the lines that start with `- ` in the form `Paper | Subject | Topic`.
