--- Ancient History (2011-2020) ---
AH Q2 (2016) [Construction of Ancient History]: Pairs: Eripatti-land revenue set apart for village tank maintenance, Taniyurs-villages donated to a single Brahmin/group of Brahmins, Ghatikas-colleges generally attached to temples. Which correctly matched? Ans: (d) 1 and 3
AH Q3 (2012) [Construction of Ancient History]: Scientific progress of Ancient India: specialised surgical instruments common by 1st century AD; organ transplant began 3rd century AD; sine of angle known 5th century AD; cyclic quadrilateral known 7th century AD. Which correct? Ans: (c) 1,3,4 only
AH Q4 (2012) [Construction of Ancient History]: Guilds (Shrenis) of ancient India: every Guild registered with central authority/King chief administrative authority; wages/rules/standards/prices fixed by Guild; Guild had judicial powers over own members. Which true? Ans: (c) 2 and 3 only
AH Q6 (2019) [Harappan Civilization]: Which one is NOT a Harappan site? (Chanhudaro/Kot Diji/Sohgaura/Desalpur) Ans: (c) Sohgaura
AH Q7 (2013) [Harappan Civilization]: Which characterises the people of the Indus Civilization? possessed great palaces/temples; worshipped both male/female deities; employed horse-drawn chariots in warfare. Ans: (b) 2 only
AH Q8 (2011) [Harappan Civilization]: Indus valley civilization: predominantly secular, religious element though present did not dominate; cotton used for manufacturing textiles. Which correct? Ans: (c) Both 1 and 2
AH Q9 (2017) [History of Vedic Period]: Difference Rigvedic Aryans vs Indus Valley people: coat of mail/helmet in warfare vs no evidence; gold/silver/copper known vs only copper/iron known; domesticated horse vs no evidence of horse. Which correct? Ans: (c) 1 and 3 only
AH Q10 (2012) [History of Vedic Period]: Religion of early Vedic Aryans primarily of? (Bhakti/Image worship and yajnas/Worship of nature and yajnas/Worship of nature and bhakti) Ans: (c) Worship of nature and yajnas
AH Q12 (2020) [History of Mauryan Age]: Ruler who advised subjects through inscription "Whosoever praises his own religious sect or blames other sects...injures his own sect very severely"? (Ashoka/Samudragupta/Harshavardhana/Krishnadeva Raya) Ans: (a) Ashoka
AH Q13 (2019) [History of Mauryan Age]: Relief sculpture inscription 'Ranyo Ashoka' (King Ashoka) with stone portrait of Ashoka found in which? (Kanganahalli/Sanchi/Shahbazgarhi/Sohgaura) Ans: (a) Kanganahalli
AH Q14 (2016) [History of Mauryan Age]: Who first deciphered the edicts of Emperor Ashoka? (Georg Buhler/James Prinsep/Max Muller/William Jones) Ans: (b) James Prinsep, 1837
AH Q17 (2012) [Buddhism and Jainism]: Common to both Buddhism and Jainism, ancient India: avoidance of extremities of penance/enjoyment; indifference to authority of Vedas; denial of efficacy of rituals. Which correct? Ans: (b) 2 and 3 only
AH Q18 (2011) [Buddhism and Jainism]: Jain philosophy holds that the world is created and maintained by? (Universal law/Universal Truth/Universal Faith/Universal Soul) Ans: (a) Universal law
AH Q20 (2020) [History of the Gupta Period]: Terms "kulyavapa" and "dronavapa" in history of India denote? (Measurement of land/Coins of different monetary value/Classification of urban land/Religious rituals) Ans: (a) Measurement of land
AH Q21 (2020) [History of the Gupta Period]: Gupta-period towns Ghantasala, Kadura and Chaul well known as? (Ports handling foreign trade/Capitals of powerful kingdoms/Places of exquisite stone art and architecture/Important Buddhist pilgrimage centres) Ans: (a) Ports handling foreign trade
AH Q22 (2019) [History of the Gupta Period]: Forced labour (Vishti) in India during Gupta period: source of income for the State, a sort of tax; totally absent in Malwa/Kathiawar regions; forced labourer entitled to weekly wages; eldest son of labourer sent as forced labourer. Correct statement? Ans: (a) It was considered a source of income for the State, a sort of tax paid by the people
AH Q23 (2013) [Harshvardhan and the Southern Dynasties]: Chinese traveller Yuan Chwang (Hiuen Tsang) recorded: roads/river-routes completely immune from robbery; mutilation, ordeals by fire/water/poison used to determine guilt; tradesmen paid duties at ferries/barrier stations. Which correct? Ans: (b) 2 and 3 only
AH Q24 (2011) [Harshvardhan and the Southern Dynasties]: Early maritime history of Bay of Bengal -- pre-eminence of India, most convincing solution: better shipbuilding technology / rulers of southern India patronised traders-priests-monks / monsoon winds facilitated sea voyages / both (a) and (b). Ans: (c) Monsoon winds across Bay of Bengal facilitated sea voyages
AH Q26 (2020) [Art and Literature during Ancient times]: Scholars/literature of ancient India: Panini associated with Pushyamitra Shunga; Amarasimha associated with Harshavardhana; Kalidasa associated with Chandra Gupta-II. Which statements correct? Ans: (c) 3 only (Kalidasa-Chandragupta II correct; Panini predates Shungas, Amarasimha linked to Chandragupta Vikramaditya not Harsha)
AH Q27 (2016) [Art and Literature during Ancient times]: Memorising chronicles/dynastic histories/epic tales was the profession of which of the following? (Shramana/Parivraajaka/Agrahaarika/Magadha) Ans: (d) Magadhas
